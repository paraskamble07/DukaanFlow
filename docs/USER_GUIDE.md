# ShopZen User Guide (English)

**Sell. Stock. Khata. Profit. Simplified.**

ShopZen keeps your entire mobile shop in your pocket — billing, stock, IMEI, customer khata (udhar), suppliers, expenses, profit and reports. Your data lives in the cloud, so it is identical on every phone you log in from.

## Daily start (30 seconds)

Open ShopZen → you see **Good Morning 👋**, today's Sales, Profit, Khata Due and Supplier Payable, plus stock alerts and pending udhar reminders.

## Make a sale (POS)

1. Tap the blue **+ NEW SALE** button (always in the centre).
2. Pick the customer (or leave *Walk-in / Cash Customer*).
3. Tap products from the bottom strip to add them to the cart. For phones, scan the IMEI and it attaches to the bill automatically.
4. **Proceed to Bill** → choose payment mode (Cash / UPI / Card / Khata-udhar), enter discount or partial amount if needed.
5. **Generate Invoice & Bill** → done. Share the bill on WhatsApp in one tap.

If you are offline, the bill is saved on the phone and syncs automatically when the internet returns — nothing is lost.

## Keep stock correct

- **Stock tab**: every product with quantity, purchase price, selling price, stock value, low-stock warnings.
- **Scan IMEI**: scan a 15-digit IMEI or barcode to instantly find a device and its full history (purchase → sale → customer → warranty).
- **New Purchase** (More → Suppliers): record stock received; quantity increases automatically and the supplier's payable is tracked.

## Khata (udhar) — give credit without fear

- **Khata tab**: every customer with total, paid and due amounts.
- Tap a customer → see full ledger → **Receive Payment** (khata auto-updates) → **WhatsApp reminder** with one tap (no WhatsApp API fees).

## Expenses & profit

- **More → Expenses**: rent, electricity, salary, transport etc. (categories pre-loaded).
- **More → Reports**: Profit & Loss, Sales, Stock (incl. dead stock), Khata, GST, Top Products, Top Customers — all computed by the backend with exact decimal money math.

## Subscription (₹30 Premium)

ShopZen Premium — **₹30/month**, one simple plan, everything included. Even if it expires, **your data is never deleted**; pay any time and everything is back.

**How to pay:**
1. Go to **More → ShopZen Premium**.
2. Scan the **QR code** with any UPI app (GooglePay/PhonePe/Paytm) and pay ₹30 (₹60 = 2 months, ₹90 = 3).
3. Copy the **UTR / reference number** from your payment receipt.
4. Type it in the app and tap **I HAVE PAID — SUBMIT**.
5. You'll see **"Payment Under Verification"** — Premium starts only after ShopZen verifies your payment (usually within a few hours). You can tap *Check Status* anytime.

## Settings

More → Settings: shop name/GSTIN/invoice prefix (used on invoices), Light/Dark theme, and language — **English / हिंदी / मराठी**.
